# Truth, knowledge, belief and visibility

## Knowledge record

KNOWLEDGE ID; information/fact ID; global truth/status; characters who know; characters who suspect; false-belief holders and contents; player knowledge; acquisition source; when learned; branch; visibility; revision/evidence.

Perspective states:
- KNOWS: supported acquisition through observation, testimony, event or approved inference.
- SUSPECTS: hypothesis, not confirmed knowledge.
- CLUE_WITHOUT_INTERPRETATION: observed evidence without its meaning.
- DOES_NOT_KNOW: established absence of acquisition in a sufficiently traced history.
- UNKNOWN: insufficient history; do not claim ignorance as a fact.

Track partial contents: knowing a murder happened does not mean knowing its perpetrator. Separate truth, what a liar said, what a listener heard and what they believe. False beliefs can persist after contrary information until belief revision is established.

## Acquisition and loss

Each acquisition has learner, information, source/witness, event/time, prerequisites and guard. Verify reachable transmission chains. Writer knowledge and unreachable overheard dialogue cannot grant information to an actor.

Distinguish human player, player character and NPC knowledge when cutscenes, dramatic irony or replay make them differ. Meta/player knowledge is not character knowledge. Amnesia, deception or forgetting needs a scoped event; preserve acquisition history.

## Query and reveal safety

For character knowledge queries select branch and time; list known information, suspicion, false beliefs and unknown coverage separately. Cite acquisitions. Without scope return common knowledge and conditional alternatives.

For safe-next-reveal queries compare approved reveal constraints, discovered clues, prerequisites and audience knowledge. Suggested reveal plans are PROPOSALS, not discoveries. Player-safe outputs omit author-only truth, hidden properties, entrances and secret identities, including leaks through headings/aliases.

Example: CHAR-005 killed the king; CHAR-006 witnessed it; CHAR-002 suspects; the player found blood without interpretation. Preserve each perspective. Writer access never advances their knowledge.
